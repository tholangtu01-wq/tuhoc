# tiengtrungdema — Nen Tang Tu Hoc Tieng Trung Ca Nhan Hoa

Ung dung hoc tieng Trung web-first tich hop quan ly da hoc vien, lo trinh hoc ca nhan hoa thong minh theo trinh do hien tai & muc tieu, cung he thong Bai Test Kiem Tra Danh Gia Nang Cap Lo Trinh tu dong.

---

## Tinh Nang Chinh

### 1. Khoi Tao Ho So & Lo Trinh Ca Nhan Hoa
- Nhap Ten/Biet danh cua hoc vien.
- Lua chon Trinh do hien tai (Moi bat dau, Can ban HSK 1-2, Trung cap HSK 3-4, Cao cap HSK 5).
- Lua chon Muc tieu mong muon (HSK 2, HSK 4, HSK 6, Hoc thuat Chuyen nganh & Luan van IMRaD).
- He thong tu dong thiet lap lo trinh rieng biet tu diem xuat phat den muc tieu mong muon.

### 2. Huong Dan Tung Buoc & Bai Test Nang Cap (Checkpoint Tests)
- Cung cap ban huong dan truc quan 4 buoc de hoc vien di dung lo trinh.
- Khi hoan thanh bai tap trong moi chang, he thong kich hoat trang thai "San Sang Thi Nang Cap".
- Hoc vien lam Bai Test Kiem Tra Nang Cap (dat >= 70%) de duoc he thong tu dong mo khoa chang tiep theo va nang trinh do len bac cao hon.

### 3. Tu Dien & So Tay Tu Vung
- Day du: Chu Han (Gian the & Phon the), Pinyin, Am Han Viet, Giai nghia, Bo thu va Chiet tu ghi nho.
- Vi du hoc thuat song ngu Trung - Viet kem phien am.
- Tinh nang So tay ca nhan (Notebook) cho phep danh dau luu lai tu vung can hoc.

### 4. Flashcards SRS (Spaced Repetition SM-2)
- Thuat toan lap lai ngat quang SM-2 toi uu hoa tri nho dai han.
- 4 cap do danh gia: Quen, Kho, Tot, De.
- The 3D lat 2 mat kem phat am tieng Trung chuan.

### 5. Luyen Viet Chu Han But Thuan
- Bang ve canvas luoi Me tu cach (Mi Zi Ge) va Dien tu cach (Tian Zi Ge).
- Che do net viet tay net mo (Ghost stroke) giup tap do theo tung net.
- Hoan tac net (Undo) va bang tong hop 7 quy tac but thuan kinh dien.

### 6. Phong Phat Am AI
- Giong doc ban ngu tieng Trung pho thong (zh-CN) voi tuy chon toc do doc.
- Nhan dien giong noi AI (Web Speech Recognition) cham diem do chinh xac theo phan tram.

### 7. Phong Thi CBT HSK & Chung Chi
- De thi trac nghiem va sap xep cau chuan HSK hoc thuat va Checkpoint 5 chang.
- Dong ho dem nguoc thoi gian thuc, tu dong cham diem va giai thich chi tiet tung cau.
- In Giay chung nhan thanh tich co in Ho ten hoc vien va ma tra cuu.

### 8. Nhat Ky Hoat Dong & Bang Diem
- Luu toan bo lich su cac de thi da lam va diem so tung lan thi rieng biet theo tung hoc vien.
- Nhat ky hoat dong chi tiet theo thoi gian thuc.
- Xuat va nhap du lieu sao luu JSON de dong bo sang thiet bi khac.
