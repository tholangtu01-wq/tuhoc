# tiengtrungdema — Nen Tang Hoc Tieng Trung Noi Bo

Ung dung hoc tieng Trung web-first tich hop he thong bao mat ma truy cap (Access Key), quan ly da hoc vien, lo trinh hoc ca nhan hoa va lich su su dung doc lap.

---

## Tinh Nang Chinh

### 1. Bao Mat Quyen Truy Cap (Access Control)
- Chi nhung nguoi duoc cap Ma Truy Cap (Access Key) moi co the mo khoa va su dung ung dung.
- Ho tro doi Ma Truy Cap linh hoat trong muc Ho So & Bao Mat.
- Mac dinh: `tiengtrungdema`, `dema2026`, `dema888`.

### 2. He Thong Ho So Hoc Vien & Lo Trinh Ca Nhan Hoa
- Moi hoc vien co Ho ten, Muc tieu hoc tap (Hoc thuat, HSK 1-2, HSK 3-4, HSK 5-6) va Avatar rieng.
- Lo trinh hoc gom 5 chang tien do mo khoa theo diem kinh nghiem (XP).
- Chuyen doi linh hoat giua cac hoc vien tren cung mot thiet bi ma khong bi de du lieu.

### 3. Tu Dien & So Tay Tu Vung
- Day du: Chu Han (Gian the & Phon the), Pinyin, Am Han Viet, Giai nghia, Bo thu va Chiet tu ghi nho.
- Vi du hoc thuat song ngu Trung - Viet kem phien am.
- Tinh nang So tay ca nhan (Notebook) cho phep danh dau luu lai tu vung can hoc.

### 4. Flashcards SRS (Spaced Repetition SM-2)
- Thuat toan lap lai ngat quang SM-2 toi uu hoa tri nho dai han.
- 4 cap do danh gia: Quen, Kho, Tot, De.
- The 3D lat 2 mat kem phat am tieng Trung chuan.

### 5. Luyen Viet Chu Han But Thuan
- Bang ve canvas luoi Me tu cach (Mi Zi Ge) va Dien tu cach (Tian Zi Ge).
- Che do net mo (Ghost stroke) giup tap do theo tung net.
- Hoan tac net (Undo) va bang tong hop 7 quy tac but thuan kinh dien.

### 6. Phong Phat Am AI
- Giong doc ban ngu tieng Trung pho thong (zh-CN) voi tuy chon toc do doc.
- Nhan dien giong noi AI (Web Speech Recognition) cham diem do chinh xac theo phan tram.

### 7. Phong Thi CBT HSK & Chung Chi
- De thi trac nghiem va sap xep cau chuan HSK hoc thuat.
- Dong ho dem nguoc thoi gian thuc, tu dong cham diem.
- In Giay chung nhan thanh tich co in Ho ten hoc vien va ma hash tra cuu.

### 8. Nhat Ky Hoat Dong & Bang Diem
- Luu toan bo lich su cac de thi da lam va diem so tung lan thi.
- Nhat ky hoat dong chi tiet theo thoi gian thuc.
- Xuat va nhap du lieu sao luu JSON de dong bo sang thiet bi khac.
